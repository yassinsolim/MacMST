# Evidence Ledger

This is MacMST's source of truth. Confidence applies to the narrowly worded claim,
not to the feasibility of native MST. `UNKNOWN` rows have no feasibility estimate.
An absent registry match is scoped to a capture, not a hardware impossibility claim.
E001-E038 preserve earlier milestone findings. ABI-02 entries E039-E052 supersede
the earlier unresolved client-lifecycle/binding/host-dispatch statements without
turning static evidence into a successful hardware experiment.
RPC-03 entries E053-E069 add lower AFK, reply-initialization, wait/cancellation
and authorization findings. They do not promote the private transport to tested.

by Git and available locally. They are selected raw technical values, not complete
registry dumps: serials, UUIDs, raw EDID, opaque property blobs, and stderr are
